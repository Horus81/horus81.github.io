How to buy

  1. Install a Solana wallet such as Phantom, then create or open your wallet. Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone, including websites or chat apps.
  2. Keep a little SOL in the wallet to pay Solana network fees.
  3. Open the official Pump.fun page linked above, or paste the mint address into Pump.fun or your wallet’s swap screen.
  4. Before you confirm, check that the mint is exactly 9RrfKqhoiXarYP5KSrQ45PYDQyRSCsstViUb55MFpump.
  5. Complete the swap in your wallet. RCS is still on the Pump.fun bonding curve and has not graduated to a standalone pool, so Pump.fun is currently the place to trade it.

A note about RCH

RCS on Solana (this page) and RCH on the XRP Ledger are separate tokens. They share a name and artwork, but they live on different ledgers and are not interchangeable. This site also hosts the XRP Ledger TOML for RCH.
